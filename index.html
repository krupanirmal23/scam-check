    What to do now

    Already tapped the link, shared a code or paid?

    Call 1930 now (National Cyber Crime Helpline), then file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.

    Call 1930

    Call your bank on the number printed on your card or on its official website, never one given in the message.

    Nothing lost, but it looks like fraud?

    Report it on Chakshu at sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc within 30 days.

    Is it about a Government of India scheme?

    Forward it to PIB Fact Check on WhatsApp: +91 8799711259.

    This is a second opinion, not a guarantee. Scam Check can be wrong. Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card number or CVV. You don't need a UPI PIN to receive money.
